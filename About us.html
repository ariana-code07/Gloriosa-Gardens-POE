About Gloriosa Gardens

Who We Are

Our Story, Mission & Vision

History

The business was founded roughly 25 years ago and has grown mainly through word of mouth and referrals.

Mission

To help clients enjoy healthier, more beautiful outdoor spaces through reliable maintenance and thoughtful, personalised garden designs.

Vision

To become the most trusted garden care and design service in the local area, known for quality work and lasting client relationships.

How We Work

Our Approach

Every garden is different, so we build our service around the four things clients tell us matter most.

Reliability

We show up on schedule, every time, so your garden care never falls through the cracks.

Craftsmanship

Twenty-five years of hands-on experience means every hedge, lawn and bed gets an experienced eye.

Eco-Conscious Care

We favour eco-friendly methods and products that keep gardens, pets and people healthy.
